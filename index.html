Zifeng Weng is a research fellow at CNRS@CREATE. His research interests include

  • Numerical simulation for renewable fuels in energy conversion processes
  • Advanced concepts and their fundamentals in propulsion systems
  • Data-driven, reduced-order modeling methods
  • Computational fluid dynamics

He obtained his Ph.D. degree in 2025 at the Center for Combustion Energy and the School of Vehicle and Mobility in Tsinghua University. He was a visiting research student in the Department of Engineering Science in University of Oxford from Sept. 2023 to Feb. 2024. From 2016 to 2020, he studied Mechanical Engineering (with honor) and Mathematics and Applied Mathematics in Shanghai Jiao Tong University.
