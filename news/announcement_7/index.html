Paper recognized as Honorable Mention in the 40th Combustion Symposium

March 01, 2025

Our recent work, Dynamics of detonation cellular structure in linear and nonlinear instability regimes, has been selected to receive an Honorable Mention in the Detonation colloquium for The Combustion Institute’s 40th International Symposium – Emphasizing Energy Transition. Link

This is the first time that Honorable Mentions have been named, making this recognition a distinguished achievement.

    Zifeng Weng, Rémy Mével*
